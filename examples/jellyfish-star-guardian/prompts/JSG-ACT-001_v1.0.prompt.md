# Prompt Record

**Asset ID:** JSG-ACT-001  
**Stage:** 08_actions  
**Version:** v1.0  
**Status:** planned

## Goal

Create a 3x3 action sheet for the approved Jellyfish Star Guardian.

The purpose is to test readable body language and personality while keeping the IP identity, outfit and materials stable.

## Locked identity

Use the approved character anchor:
- transparent blue-violet jellyfish dome
- 3–5 warm star inserts
- lavender hair
- blue-violet eyes
- compact Q-version proportions
- same blue-violet base outfit
- same shoes
- same 3D collectible-toy material language

## Change / explore

Change:
- body pose
- limb direction
- center of gravity
- small facial reaction
- very small action-specific accessory only when essential

Do not change:
- outfit
- face construction
- head shape
- body proportions
- material system
- main palette

## Nine actions

1. walking while looking around
2. running / small quick step
3. floating upward
4. crouching to observe something
5. reaching forward to guide a small creature
6. turning back while moving
7. sitting and resting
8. tiptoe / balancing
9. opening arms in a welcoming gesture

## Canonical prompt

```text
Create a clean 3x3 action sheet of ONE identical 3D collectible-toy jellyfish guardian girl.

LOCK CHARACTER:
same transparent blue-violet jellyfish dome;
same 3–5 warm star inserts;
same lavender hair;
same blue-violet eyes;
same face proportions;
same compact Q-version body;
same blue-violet base outfit;
same shoes;
same materials;
same character scale.

CHANGE ONLY:
body action;
limb direction;
center of gravity;
small facial reaction;
very small action-specific gesture.

NINE ACTIONS:
1 walking while looking around
2 small quick run
3 floating upward
4 crouching to observe
5 reaching forward to guide
6 turning back while moving
7 sitting and resting
8 tiptoe / balancing
9 opening arms in welcome

POSE QUALITY:
clear readable silhouettes;
natural balance for a chibi body;
avoid impossible joints;
avoid tangled limbs;
hands and feet must remain simple and anatomically believable.

BACKGROUND:
simple pale blue-violet studio field;
no full environment;
no scenery;
no text;
no decorative props.

RENDERING:
premium 3D collectible-toy product render;
painted PVC / ABS for opaque parts;
clear resin / transparent PVC for the jellyfish dome;
soft studio lighting.

OUTPUT:
3:4 board;
3x3 grid;
same character scale in all cells.
```

## Negative / Do not

- no outfit changes
- no large props
- no umbrellas
- no shell throne
- no coral scene
- no moon scene
- no complex background
- no extra companion character unless essential for one guiding pose
- no 2D illustration
- no text

## Repair rule

If the poses become too similar, change center of gravity and silhouette first.
If the grid becomes busy, remove props and environment before changing the character.
