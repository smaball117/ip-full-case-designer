# Prompt Record

**Asset ID:** JSG-OUT-001  
**Stage:** 09_outfits  
**Version:** v0.1  
**Status:** rejected

## Goal

Test a 3x3 themed outfit series from the approved character.

## Why rejected

The series changed props and scenes but many outfits remained too similar.
Backgrounds became full fantasy scenes and competed with the character.

## Canonical prompt used

```text
Create a 3x3 themed collectible-toy outfit grid for the approved jellyfish guardian girl.
Keep the transparent star-filled jellyfish dome and face unchanged.
Create nine ocean / dream / travel themed looks with matching props and fantasy backgrounds.
Use blue-violet, pink, aqua and gold accents.
Each cell should feel rich and atmospheric.
```

## Failure
- "rich and atmospheric" encouraged clutter
- outfit silhouettes repeated
- too many background objects
- scenes became more memorable than clothing

## Repair delta
- remove "rich" and "full fantasy scene"
- require at least 2–3 outfit dimensions to change
- max 3 background element categories
- lower background contrast / detail
- keep character identity locked
