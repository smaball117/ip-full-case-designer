## Goal

Create a 3x3 expression sheet for the approved Jellyfish Star Guardian while preserving the exact 3D collectible-toy identity.

The sheet should test personality, not redesign the character.

## Locked identity

- transparent rounded jellyfish dome
- 3–5 warm glowing stars suspended inside the dome
- lavender hair
- large blue-violet eyes
- compact Q-version body
- blue-violet short layered dress
- real-world toy material logic
- smooth simplified collectible-toy forms

## Canonical prompt

```text
Create a clean 3x3 expression sheet of the approved 3D collectible-toy jellyfish guardian girl.

LOCK CHARACTER:
same large transparent rounded jellyfish dome;
same soft scalloped rim;
same 3–5 warm cream-yellow star inserts inside the dome;
same lavender short hair;
same large blue-violet eyes;
same compact Q-version body;
same blue-violet short layered dress;
same painted-PVC + clear-resin / transparent-PVC toy material logic;
same smooth simplified molded forms.

CHANGE ONLY:
facial expression and very small body/head gesture.

NINE EXPRESSIONS:
1. cheerful greeting
2. big laugh
3. teary / sad
4. tiny angry pout
5. surprised
6. affectionate / heart gesture
7. sleepy / drowsy
8. proud / approval
9. curious / confused

EXPRESSION METHOD:
change eyebrow angle, eye openness, mouth shape, blush and small head tilt;
keep the face construction consistent;
do not redesign the eyes or head shape.

COMPOSITION:
3x3 grid;
3:4 total board;
same character scale in every cell;
clean spacing;
simple pale blue-violet studio background;
very low background detail;
no scene props;
no captions or labels.

MATERIAL:
matte painted PVC for face/body/hair;
clear resin or transparent PVC for jellyfish dome;
small warm star inserts inside the dome;
commercial collectible-toy product rendering.

VISUAL PRIORITY:
face first;
jellyfish dome second;
body gesture third.

OUTPUT:
highly consistent 3D collectible-toy expression board, clean and production-like.
```

## Negative / Do not

- no outfit changes
- no new props
- no full scene background
- no text
- no anime-style face redesign
- no changing eye color or head/body ratio
- no extra jewelry or tentacles
- no 2D illustration rendering

## Output

- Ratio: 3:4
- Grid / count: 3x3 / 9 expressions
- Background: simple pale blue-violet studio field
- Text policy: none
- Material / rendering: collectible-toy 3D product render

## Repair notes

If expressions make the character drift:
1. restore face proportions first;
2. reduce eye-shape variation;
3. use eyebrows + mouth + head tilt for emotion;
4. keep dome and stars completely unchanged.

If the board feels busy:
1. remove all background props;
2. use one simple background tone with soft lighting;
3. keep equal spacing between cells.

## Learnings

Expression-sheet variety should come from facial cues and small gestures, not new accessories or scene changes.
