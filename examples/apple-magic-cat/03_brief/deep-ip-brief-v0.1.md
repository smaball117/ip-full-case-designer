# Apple Magic Cat — Deep IP Brief v0.1

Status: **Stage 03 complete / ready for sketch exploration**

## 01. Name

**可可 / Coco**

Working English name: **Coco**

The English lockup is not final yet. Naming typography will be explored later in the Visual Identity stage.

## 02. First impression

**可爱、甜蜜。**

The tone should feel sweet and affectionate without becoming childish, sugary, or overly decorative.

## 03. Core personality

- 勇敢
- 贪吃
- 粘人

### Personality logic

**勇敢** is the action engine: when a wish needs help, Coco is willing to step forward.

**粘人** is the emotional engine: after being brave, Coco still wants to stay close to familiar people, objects, or companions.

**贪吃** is the comic flaw and connects naturally to the limited dessert-magic behavior.

This replaces the earlier "timid / quiet" personality proposal. The selected world premise remains, but the character personality is updated by the user's brief.

## 04. Useful flaw

**贪吃** is currently treated as the primary flaw.

Coco can be brave when helping with wishes, but may get distracted by food or secretly taste a tiny part of a celebration treat.

This is a working assumption based on the user's answers and can be revised later without changing the world premise.

## 05. Likes

**苹果**

Apple remains both a favorite object and the primary world symbol.

## 06. Dislikes

**香蕉**

Current interpretation: banana is a disliked food/object.

This can later become a small recurring joke, but should not become a second major symbol.

## 07. Why Coco collects wishes

It is a **task from the Magic Academy**.

Coco is a magic-academy apprentice assigned to collect and care for wishes through special apples.

This gives the character:
- a reason to perform the job;
- room for academy rules, badges, assignments and seasonal stories;
- a natural reason to make mistakes while still learning.

## 08. Apple rule

The apple is a **wish collector**.

Confirmed rules:
- a wish / task is collected inside an apple;
- when one complete thing is successfully finished, **a star appears inside the apple**;
- when the attempt fails, **the apple turns green**.

Not locked yet:
- the apple's initial state before success or failure;
- whether a green failed apple can be retried;
- whether stars accumulate or each apple corresponds to one completed wish;
- what happens to the apple after the star is created.

These should be resolved only if they matter to story or design.

## 09. Dessert magic

Level: **A — very occasional**

Dessert magic is not a second identity.

It appears only as a small celebration ritual after a wish or task succeeds, for example:
- tiny candy;
- mini apple pie;
- tiny biscuit;
- small edible gift.

The dessert element must remain accessory-scale and emotionally meaningful.

## 10. Target audience

**年轻女性**

Implication:
- cute but not juvenile;
- emotionally warm but not overly sweet;
- collectible rather than children's-cartoon coded;
- suitable for desk objects, gifting, social stickers and lifestyle merchandise.

## 11. Priority applications

- 手办
- 毛绒
- 表情包
- 文创

This means the character must work in:
- 3D collectible form;
- simplified plush silhouette;
- readable facial-expression system;
- 2D graphic and print applications.

## 12. Avoid

- 太幼稚
- 太日漫
- 太复杂
- 太甜腻

### Visual implications

Avoid:
- oversized anime eyes;
- excessive lace / ribbons / dessert decoration;
- too many props;
- complicated costume construction;
- children's-cartoon proportions;
- high-sugar pink overload.

Prefer:
- simplified designer-toy geometry;
- restrained apple red;
- cream white base;
- soft pink as support rather than dominant color;
- small expressive eyebrows and mouth;
- one clear primary symbol at a time.

# One-line IP premise

**可可是一只在魔法学院学习收集愿望的奶油白小猫。它把人们没有说出口的小愿望收进苹果里，当一件事情真正完成时，苹果里会亮起一颗星星；如果失败，苹果就会变成青色。勇敢又粘人的可可总会继续陪在愿望旁边，而成功之后，它偶尔会偷偷用一点魔法变出小小的甜点庆祝。**

## Core identity hierarchy

1. Coco / 可可
2. Wish-collecting apple
3. Star created by completion
4. Magic Academy apprentice identity
5. Brave + clingy + gluttonous personality
6. Occasional celebration dessert magic

## What remains intentionally open

- exact academy name;
- exact apple lifecycle beyond success/failure;
- whether Coco has classmates or supporting characters;
- final English naming / logo lockup;
- exact costume form;
- final 3D proportions.

These are not blockers for Stage 04 sketch exploration.
