# Decision Log

## 2026-09-21 — Gate A concept selection

**Stage:** Concept Direction  
**Decision:** Use Direction A — Wish Keeper as the core concept, with a limited amount of Direction E — Dessert Magician behavior.

**Why:**  
The Wish Keeper route gives the red apple a strong emotional narrative role. Dessert magic adds small celebratory actions and commercial extension without replacing the core world premise.

**Rejected alternatives:**  
B, C, D, and E as standalone primary worlds.

**What becomes locked:**  
- Wish Keeper is the primary narrative role.
- Red apple is primarily a wish collector / container.
- Dessert magic is secondary and appears mainly after a wish or task succeeds.
- The character should not become a pastry-chef or food-only mascot.

**What remains open:**  
- exact academy name;
- exact apple lifecycle beyond success/failure;
- supporting characters;
- final English naming / logo lockup.

## 2026-09-21 — Deep IP Brief

**Stage:** Deep IP Brief  
**Decision:** Name the character 可可 / Coco. Core personality is brave, gluttonous, and clingy. The Magic Academy assigns the wish-collection task. Apples collect wishes; completion creates a star inside; failure turns the apple green. Dessert magic remains rare. Primary audience is young women. Priority applications are figures, plush, stickers/emoji, and cultural/creative merchandise.

**Why:**  
This gives Coco a clearer emotional and behavioral engine while keeping the apple symbol functional rather than decorative. The audience and avoid-list support a collectible, designer-toy direction rather than a children's-cartoon or anime-heavy direction.

**Working assumptions:**  
- "贪吃" is used as the primary useful flaw.
- "苹果" is treated as the favorite.
- "香蕉" is treated as a dislike.
- Initial apple state and retry mechanics are intentionally left open.

**What becomes locked for sketch exploration:**  
- name: 可可 / Coco;
- core personality: 勇敢 / 贪吃 / 粘人;
- apple = wish collector;
- success = star appears inside;
- failure = apple turns green;
- Magic Academy task premise;
- dessert magic level A;
- audience = young women;
- avoid childish / anime-heavy / complex / overly sugary styling.

**What remains open:**  
- final character silhouette;
- exact costume and hat form;
- exact accessory placement;
- final 3D proportion;
- final logo and typography.
