# Decision Log

## 2026-09-21 — Gate A concept selection

**Stage:** Concept Direction  
**Decision:** Use Direction A — Wish Keeper as the core concept, with a limited amount of Direction E — Dessert Magician behavior.

**Why:**  
The Wish Keeper route best matches the character's current gentle, quiet, soft-vinyl temperament and gives the red apple a strong emotional narrative role. Dessert magic adds small celebratory actions and commercial extension without replacing the core world premise.

**Rejected alternatives:**  
B, C, D, and E as standalone primary worlds.

**What becomes locked:**  
- Wish Keeper is the primary narrative role.
- Red apple is primarily a wish container.
- Dessert magic is secondary and appears mainly after a wish matures or comes true.
- The character should not become a pastry-chef or food-only mascot.

**What remains open:**  
- final character name;
- exact world rules;
- target age/audience;
- magic limitations;
- visual symbol hierarchy;
- wording of the final IP statement.
