## Important visual corrections learned from testing

1. Coco must read clearly as a cat, not a bear.
2. Large cat pupils + star-shaped highlights are important identity cues.
3. Props should remain small and secondary to the face.
4. The current preferred 3D material is flocked / velvety.
5. Flat graphics should stay clean, with orange-red fine outlines and solid fills.
6. Posters should not become crowded just because the reference is busy.
7. The user does not want random small text.
8. When a poster looks too close to a reference, changing only small details is insufficient. The theme/scene structure must be redesigned.
