# Apple Magic Cat — Selected Concept Direction

Status: **Gate A approved**

## Core route

**Primary:** Direction A — Wish Keeper / 愿望收藏师  
**Secondary influence:** Direction E — Apple Dessert Magician / 苹果甜点魔术师

## Final concept logic

Apple Magic Cat is primarily a **Wish Keeper**.

It collects wishes through special apples as part of a Magic Academy assignment. The apple is the core super-symbol and main narrative object.

The Dessert Magician influence is used only as an **occasional celebration behavior**, not as a second world premise.

When a wish or task succeeds, Coco may use a tiny amount of apple magic to create a miniature treat or gift. These are emotional rewards, not the main purpose of the apples.

## One-line premise

可可是一只在魔法学院学习收集愿望的奶油白小猫。它把愿望收进苹果里，一件事情真正完成时，苹果里会出现一颗星星；失败时苹果会变成青色。成功之后，可可偶尔会用一点苹果魔法变出小小的甜点庆祝。

## Character role

愿望收藏师 / 魔法学院学徒。

## Personality direction

- 勇敢
- 贪吃
- 粘人

Earlier "timid / quiet" personality wording is superseded by the user's Deep Brief.

## Apple meaning

**Primary:** wish collector / wish container.  
**Success state:** a star appears inside after a completed task or wish.  
**Failure state:** the apple turns green.

Apple must never be reduced to an ordinary food prop.

## Dessert behavior

Very occasional.

Dessert elements should be:
- miniature;
- accessory-scale;
- celebratory;
- emotionally meaningful.

Avoid turning Coco into:
- a pastry chef;
- a café-only mascot;
- a food-brand-only character;
- a scene overloaded with cakes, utensils, or kitchen props.

## Audience implication

Primary audience: young women.

The character should be cute and sweet without becoming:
- childish;
- anime-heavy;
- visually complicated;
- excessively sugary.
