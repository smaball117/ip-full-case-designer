# Apple Magic Cat — Selected Concept Direction

Status: **Gate A approved**

## Core route

**Primary:** Direction A — Wish Keeper / 愿望收藏师  
**Secondary influence:** Direction E — Apple Dessert Magician / 苹果甜点魔术师

## Final concept logic

Apple Magic Cat is primarily a **Wish Keeper**.

It collects small wishes that people have not said out loud and stores them inside red apples. The red apple remains the core super-symbol and the main narrative object.

The Dessert Magician influence is used only as a **celebration behavior**, not as a second world premise.

When a wish becomes ready to bloom or has just come true, the cat can use a tiny piece of apple magic to create a miniature celebratory sweet, snack, or gift. These are emotional rewards and ritual objects, not the main purpose of the apples.

## One-line premise

一只负责收集“没有被说出口的小愿望”的奶油白猫魔法学徒，把愿望保存在红苹果里；当愿望成熟或实现时，它会用一点苹果魔法变出小小的甜点或礼物，替人庆祝。

## Character role

愿望收藏师 / 小型愿望保管员 / 偶尔会做庆祝魔法的小学徒。

## Personality direction

- 温柔
- 有点胆小
- 很认真
- 好奇
- 被夸时会偷偷得意
- 对甜食有一点点贪心

## Useful flaw

它总想先照顾好别人的愿望，经常忘记自己的愿望；而且每次做庆祝甜点都说“只尝一口”，最后往往会偷吃一点。

## Apple meaning

**Primary meaning:** 愿望容器。  
**Secondary behavior:** 当愿望成熟后，可释放一小部分魔力变成庆祝甜点、糖果、苹果派、小饼干或礼物。

Apple must never be reduced to an ordinary food prop.

## Recurring behaviors

- 把耳朵贴近苹果听愿望
- 给苹果系蝴蝶结并分类
- 用魔法棒检查愿望是否成熟
- 愿望实现后，把一部分苹果魔法变成小甜点或小礼物
- 偷偷试吃庆祝甜点
- 把实现后的愿望放飞成小星星

## Visual implication

Keep the current gentle, quiet, soft-vinyl character tone.

Dessert elements should be:
- miniature;
- occasional;
- round and simplified;
- accessory-scale;
- emotionally meaningful.

Avoid turning the character into:
- a pastry chef;
- a café mascot;
- a food-brand-only character;
- a scene overloaded with cakes, utensils, or kitchen props.

## Commercial extension

Primary:
- wish cards
- wishing apple charms
- birthday / holiday gifting
- emotion stickers
- wish-exchange pop-up

Secondary:
- celebratory mini dessert series
- seasonal gift boxes
- café or dessert collaborations
- limited "wish fulfilled" packaging

## Gate A result

**Approved concept:** A-led hybrid with limited E behavior.
